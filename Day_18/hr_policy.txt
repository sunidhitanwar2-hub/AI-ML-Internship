COMPANY HR POLICY

1. Work From Home:
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

2. Leave Policy:
Employees receive 12 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves per year.

3. Working Hours:
The standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.

4. Attendance:
Employees must inform their manager if they are unable to attend work.

5. Holidays:
The company follows the official holiday calendar.
