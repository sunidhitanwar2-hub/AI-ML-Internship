NovaTech Solutions - Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
(Sample company documentation created for this project. NovaTech Solutions is a fictional company.)

1. Overview
NovaTech follows a hybrid work model. Most employees work from the office on some days and from home on the other days. This policy explains who can work remotely, the rules for hybrid work, and the equipment and allowances the company provides.

2. Hybrid Work Schedule
Employees in hybrid roles must work from the office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are "anchor days," when all team members are expected to be in the office for meetings and collaboration. The third office day can be chosen by the employee in agreement with their manager.
Office hours are flexible, but all employees must be available during core hours, which are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, whether they are working from home or the office.

3. Eligibility
Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing their probation period of 3 months. During probation, new employees work from the office 5 days a week so they can learn the team's processes and build relationships.
Some roles, such as lab engineers, facilities staff and front desk staff, are office-only roles and are not eligible for hybrid work.

4. Fully Remote Work
Fully remote work is allowed only in special cases, such as a medical condition or relocation for family reasons. A fully remote arrangement must be approved by the department head and HR, and it is reviewed every 6 months.

5. Work From Anywhere
Hybrid employees can work from a location other than their home city for up to 15 working days per year. They must inform their manager at least 1 week in advance. Working from outside India is not allowed because of tax and data security rules, unless approved in writing by HR and the IT security team.

6. Home Office Setup
The company provides every hybrid and remote employee with a laptop, a headset and a laptop stand. Employees can also claim a one-time home office allowance of Rs. 10,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. The claim must be submitted within 60 days of becoming eligible for hybrid work, along with the purchase bills.

7. Internet Allowance
Hybrid and remote employees receive an internet allowance of Rs. 1,500 per month, paid along with the monthly salary. No bills are required for the internet allowance.

8. Security While Working Remotely
When working outside the office, employees must connect to the company VPN before accessing any internal system. Employees must not work on company data using public Wi-Fi networks (for example in cafes, airports or hotels) without the VPN. Laptops must be locked when not in use, and confidential calls should not be taken in public places. More details are in the IT Security Policy.

9. Attendance
Office attendance is recorded automatically through the ID card at the entrance. Managers review attendance every month. Employees who do not meet the 3-day office requirement for 2 months in a row will receive a written reminder, and repeated issues may lead to the hybrid arrangement being withdrawn.

10. Contact
Questions about hybrid work can be sent to the HR team at hr@novatech.example.
